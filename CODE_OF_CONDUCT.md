# Code of Conduct

Soloe's community should be a place where people can discuss developer tooling, report failures, and contribute without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still taking reported impact seriously.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's source code, credentials, logs, or personal information.
- Make space for people with different experience levels, platforms, and communication styles.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, or retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces or in public interactions representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed in context and may result in content removal, warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, community spaces, and public events where someone is representing Soloe.
